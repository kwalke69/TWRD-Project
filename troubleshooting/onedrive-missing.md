# 🔎 OneDrive Is Missing from File Explorer

You may not be signed in, or OneDrive may not be running or configured.

## Fix

1. Open the Start menu, search for **OneDrive**, and open the app.
2. Sign in with your Microsoft or organization account and finish setup. See [Sign In](../README.md#31-sign-in) for screenshots.
3. Reopen File Explorer and check the navigation pane.

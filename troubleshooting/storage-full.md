# 💾 OneDrive Storage Is Full

## Fix

1. Review your storage usage.
2. Move important files elsewhere before deleting anything.
3. Delete unneeded files and empty the OneDrive recycle bin.
4. Contact your IT department if you need more storage.

> [!WARNING]
> Deleting a file from OneDrive can also remove it from your other synced devices.

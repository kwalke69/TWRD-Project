# ⬇️ Files Will Not Download

## Fix

1. Confirm your internet connection and that you are signed in. See [3.1 Sign In](../README.md#31-sign-in) if needed.
2. Check free storage on your computer. To clear space, see [4.2 Free Up Local Storage](../README.md#42-free-up-local-storage).
3. Confirm the file still exists and you still have access.
4. Try again with [4.1 Download Files and Keep Them Offline](../README.md#41-download-files-and-keep-them-offline).

> [!NOTE]
> Online-only files need an internet connection to download and open.
